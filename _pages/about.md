<!-- # About -->

My name is Sharath Ram Kumar, and I am a Research Fellow at the Nanyang Technological University in Singapore, working in the [Descartes Research Project](https://descartes.cnrsatcreate.cnrs.fr/). I recently defended my PhD thesis on [Knowledge-Informed Reinforcement Learning for Building Energy Management](https://dr.ntu.edu.sg/entities/publication/5e2079b5-960a-42ac-b032-bbeaa83a5d7b), through a joint PhD program between NTU and the Université Grenoble-Alpes in France. I was jointly supervised by Prof Arvind Easwaran at NTU, Prof Benoit Delinchant at UGA and Dr Rémy Rigo-Mariani at the CNRS in France.

My work focuses on bringing advanced control techniques, specifically reinforcement learning, into real-world control tasks in buildings. While several pilot studies and trials have shown their promise in controlled settings, my research seeks to develop methodologies that consider practical limitations such as scalability, limited data access, and unpredictable human behaviour.
